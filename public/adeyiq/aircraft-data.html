AKE 1,588 kg and PMC 6,804 kg are standard ULD ratings; PGA 11,340 kg is the 20 ft pallet rating. Lower-deck pallet and AKH limits are typical working values. Cargo longer than a PMC goes on a PGA (20 ft) or on two connected pallets on a freighter main deck.
