Engines & landing gear · will it fit?

AircraftResultWhy

Landing gear sizes are rough planning sizes for a gear leg in its shipping fixture with the wheels off; they are not manufacturer data, so enter the real fixture size. Bare-engine size and dry weight are approximate public manufacturer figures. The in-stand size and weight are estimates (the stand adds about 60 cm length, 35 cm width, 55 cm height and 20% + 500 kg). Main-deck engines are planned on a centreline position, limited by the door height. When no aircraft fits, the engine needs a special freighter or a split into modules: check with the engine shop; replace them with the stand drawing from the engine shop. Doors, contours and pallet limits are working values; position and floor limits come from the airline. Planning aid only.
