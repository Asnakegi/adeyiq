About AdeyIQ

Named after the flower of new beginnings

Adey Abeba is the yellow flower that covers the Ethiopian highlands each September, when the new year begins. AdeyIQ takes its name and its colour from it: a fresh start for how cargo is planned, with intelligence behind every decision.

Why it matters

  • Cargo is growing faster than ramp teams. Boeing expects air cargo traffic to double by 2043, and Airbus sees 45% more freighters by 2044. The same teams will have to build far more ULDs every night.
  • Every empty position is lost revenue. When a ULD is built by eye, space inside the contour goes unused and the flight leaves with less paid cargo than it could carry.
  • Safety cannot depend on memory. DG segregation, cold-chain cargo and heavy pieces need the same checks on every shift, at 3 a.m. as much as at noon.
  • Africa is where growth is fastest. IATA reported African air cargo demand up 21% in February 2026. AdeyIQ is built for airlines and handlers ready to capture that growth.

AdeyIQ brings smart build-up, revenue optimization and a live capacity marketplace into one place, so every ULD is planned before it reaches the ramp and every flight leaves as full and as safe as it can be.

45% more freighters by 2044

3.3%cargo growth a year
3,420freighters in 2044

Airbus expects air cargo to grow faster than world trade, at 3.3% a year against 2.7%. It sees the freighter fleet growing 45%, with 1,670 passenger-to-freighter conversions and 935 new-build freighters. Asia-Pacific and North America take nearly two-thirds of the demand.

More freighters mean more main-deck pallets to build well. Build your cargo virtually handles PMC and Q6 contours today.

Air cargo traffic set to double by 2043

4%growth a year

Boeing's World Air Cargo Forecast sees traffic growing about 4% a year, doubling over 20 years. The freighter fleet grows from 2,340 in 2023 to about 3,900, and express carriers rise from 18% to a quarter of the market. Emerging markets lead the growth.

Double the cargo with the same ramp teams means every build has to be right the first time.
