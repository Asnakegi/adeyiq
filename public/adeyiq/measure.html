Piece to measure
Measure the maximum outside size, as the piece will be handled. Include anything that sticks out, wrapping and straps, and the pallet or skid if it travels with the piece. Round up to the next whole cm.

Camera estimate

  1. Put a ruler, tape or object of known length flat against the cargo, along the edge you measure and in the same plane.
  2. Stand back and take the photo straight on, square to that face, with the whole edge in the picture.
  3. Tap both ends of the reference, then both ends of the cargo edge.

The result is an estimate from the photo, not an exact or 3D measurement. Use a tape instead when the reference can't lie along the edge, the piece is irregular, or the fit is tight.
