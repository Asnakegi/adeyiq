Privacy

This page describes what AdeyIQ (adeyiq.vercel.app) collects and why. It covers the home page, the Cargo Builder (/build) and the other pages on this site.

Cargo you enter stays on your device

Shipments you type or upload in the Cargo Builder are saved only in your own browser (local storage). That includes AWB numbers, pieces, sizes, weights and DG codes. They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them. The one exception is a measurement request, described next.

Measurement requests

When a planner uses "Request measurement" for a piece, AdeyIQ's server stores that request so that warehouse staff can open it on a phone or tablet and send the measured size back. The server keeps only:

  • the AWB, the planner's piece reference (for example "Piece 2 of 3") and the planned length, width and height in cm, where known
  • once sent: the measured length, width and height, whether each was taped or estimated from a photo, an optional short note, and the time

Photos taken for a camera estimate stay on the phone; they are never uploaded. No names, accounts or IP addresses are stored with a request. The link contains a random code that only the people you share it with have; it opens and can send sizes for that one piece only, once. An unanswered request is deleted automatically when its link expires (4 hours to 3 days, chosen by the planner). A sent result is kept for up to 3 days so the planner can review it, and is deleted as soon as the planner applies or removes it. Requests are stored with Upstash (Redis), our storage provider for this feature.

Usage counts

To see how the site is used, each page sends a short event to our server when it opens and when you use main tools such as add cargo, upload or load aircraft. Each event contains:

  • a random visitor id created in your browser (not linked to your name), plus a random id for the current visit
  • the page, the action and a short status, such as how many ULDs were built or whether the load is balanced (never AWB numbers, goods, dimensions, weights or DG details)
  • the aircraft type selected, your screen width (to tell phone, tablet or desktop apart) and the website that linked you here (its address only, not the full link)
  • the country, region and city of your network, as provided by our hosting provider

We do not store your IP address or the cargo details you enter. Events from known automated scanners are ignored.

Access requests

If you send the "Request access" form, we receive what you type: your name, email, company, phone, the modules you are interested in and your message, plus the country and city of your network. We use it only to reply to you about AdeyIQ.

Feedback

If you answer the "Rate AdeyIQ" questions, we receive your answers, the role you chose, your comment (up to 150 characters) and the same visitor id as above.

Where the data is kept

ServiceWhat it does
VercelHosts the site and provides the network location. Like any web host, it keeps short-term request logs. Vercel Web Analytics also counts page views (page address, no cookies).
Google (Sheets and Apps Script)Stores usage events, access requests and feedback in a private spreadsheet only the AdeyIQ team can open, and sends an email notice for each access request.
UpstashStores measurement requests and their results until they expire or are applied (see above). Nothing else is stored there.
Google FontsDelivers the fonts. Your browser downloads them directly, so Google sees your IP address. The spreadsheet reader for Excel uploads is served by AdeyIQ itself and runs in your browser.

Cookies

AdeyIQ does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for the random visitor id and your saved cargo.

Your choices

  • The Clear button in the Cargo Builder deletes the cargo saved in this browser. On a shared computer, use it when you finish. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes your saved cargo and resets the visitor id.
  • Printing, saving as PDF and saving as HTML happen in your browser: the documents are not sent to AdeyIQ.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or to have your access request or feedback deleted, send the Request access form on the home page with "Privacy request" in the message. We reply to the email you give.

We have not yet set a fixed period for keeping these records. Until we do, they are kept until you ask us to delete them or we no longer need them.
